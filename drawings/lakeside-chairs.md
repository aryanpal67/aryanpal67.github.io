This is one of my favourite paintings! 🎨 I painted **two wooden chairs sitting by a beautiful mountain lake**. I love how peaceful it looks — like you could just sit down, watch the mountains and listen to the water.

I used lots of blue and green colours for the sky, lake and pine trees, and warm yellow and orange for the wooden chairs. The white fluffy clouds were my favourite part to paint!

**What I used:** Watercolour paints and black markers for the outlines.

The yellow flowers in the front and the stepping stones on the path make it feel like a real place I'd love to visit someday. 🌼🏔️

Would you like to sit in one of those chairs and watch the mountains? I know I would! 😊
