This is my **RAINBOW WORLD** drawing! I used lots and lots of colored pencils to create this magical place full of colors. Every color of the rainbow is here – red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet!

I drew:
- 🌈 A beautiful rainbow stretching across the sky
- 🌸 Colorful flowers blooming everywhere
- ☀️ A bright, happy sun
- ☁️ Fluffy clouds
- 🦋 Pretty butterflies flying around
- 🌳 Green trees and plants

This is my happy place! When I feel sad, I think about my rainbow world and it makes me smile. What colours would you add to it? 🎨💫
