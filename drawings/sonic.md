This is my drawing of **Sonic the Hedgehog** — the fastest thing alive! 💙⚡

I love Sonic so much because he is super fast and really cool. I drew him giving a thumbs up, which is his signature move! I used blue crayon for his spiky fur and red for his iconic sneakers.

The hardest part was getting his spiky hair just right — Sonic's quills are very tricky to draw! I practiced many times before I got it looking good.

**Fun fact:** Sonic can run faster than the speed of sound — that's over 1,200 km per hour! 🚀

Do you like Sonic too? He is definitely my favourite video game character! 💙
