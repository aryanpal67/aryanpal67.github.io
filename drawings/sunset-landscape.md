This painting shows a **beautiful golden sunset over mountains** with a winding river path through the middle. 🌅

I used so many warm colours — pink, orange, yellow, purple and brown — to make the sky glow like a real sunset. The mountains in the background are purple and blue, and the trees in the front are painted with dark brown colours.

**My favourite things about this painting:**
- 🌄 The glowing white sun peeking between the two mountains
- 🌊 The shiny river path that catches the golden light
- 🌳 The tall bare trees on the left and the leafy tree on the right
- 🟣 The purple and lavender mountain layers getting lighter in the distance

This took me a long time because I had to wait for each layer of watercolour to dry before adding the next one. But it was totally worth it! 😊

I think this is one of the best landscapes I have ever painted. What do you think? 🎨
