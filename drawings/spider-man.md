This is my drawing of **Spider-Man** — your friendly neighbourhood superhero! 🕷️❤️

Spider-Man is definitely one of the coolest superheroes ever. I drew a close-up of his iconic mask with those big white eyes and the famous spider-web pattern all over his face. Drawing the web lines was the hardest part — I had to be very careful to make them go in curved lines across the whole head!

I used red and pink crayons for his mask and black marker for all the web lines. His yellow suit underneath adds a nice bright contrast.

**Fun fact:** Spider-Man's real name is Peter Parker, a high school student who was bitten by a radioactive spider! 🕸️

With great power comes great responsibility — that's Spider-Man's most famous rule! ❤️🕷️
