This is my drawing of **Knuckles the Echidna** — the guardian of the Master Emerald! 👊❤️

Knuckles is red, strong, and super serious about protecting his emerald. I drew him in his classic tough-guy pose. The most challenging part was drawing his famous spiked knuckles on both fists — they look like little horns and I had to draw them very carefully!

I used deep red crayon for almost his entire body. His dark eyes and serious expression make him look really tough and determined.

**Fun fact:** Knuckles is so strong he can punch through solid rock and even shatter metal with his fists! 💪

He and Sonic are rivals but also friends — they always end up working together to save the world! ❤️
