This is my drawing of **Shadow the Hedgehog** — the ultimate life form! 🖤❤️

Shadow is the darkest and most powerful character in the Sonic universe. I drew him in his battle stance — wide legs, clenched fists, and that famous fierce expression! I used grey and black for his fur and added his red streaks and yellow power rings on his wrists and ankles.

Shadow is much harder to draw than Sonic because of all the red details and his angry face. But I love how it turned out — he looks really powerful!

**Fun fact:** Shadow was created 50 years ago by Dr Gerald Robotnik and was frozen in space until Sonic woke him up! 🚀

He might look mean but Shadow is actually a hero deep down! 🖤
