This is my drawing of **Miles 'Tails' Prower** — Sonic's best friend! 🦊💛

Tails is a two-tailed fox who can fly by spinning his tails like a helicopter. I drew him in his classic pose, ready for action! I used yellow and orange crayons for his fluffy fur, and his bright teal eyes are my favourite part.

I also gave him his red and white sneakers — just like in the Sonic games! Getting the two tails right was challenging but I think it turned out really well.

**Fun fact:** Tails's real name is Miles Prower — say it fast and it sounds like "miles per hour"! 😄

Tails is a super smart inventor and Sonic's most trusted friend. 🔧💛
