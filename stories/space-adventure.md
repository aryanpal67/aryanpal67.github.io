## Blast Off!

My name is Zara, and I'm an astronaut! 🚀 I fly spaceships and explore distant planets. On this particular day, I was on a mission to discover a brand new planet that had never been visited before.

My rocket was built with the latest technology – shiny silver panels, glowing buttons, and a computer that could think almost as fast as I could! I counted down:

**"5... 4... 3... 2... 1... BLAST OFF!"**

The engines roared, and up, up, up I went, leaving Earth far behind. The stars twinkled like diamonds in the black sky.

## The Unexpected Landing

I had been flying for what felt like forever when my computer started beeping urgently.

**BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!**

"Oh no!" I said. "What's wrong, Computer?"

"Engine trouble ahead, Zara," the computer said in a calm voice. "Prepare for emergency landing on nearest planet."

Through my window, I saw the most amazing sight – a **PURPLE PLANET** with swirling pink clouds! It was beautiful, but I was nervous. Would it be safe?

WHOOOOSH! BANG! The rocket touched down on the purple ground, and a cloud of purple dust flew everywhere.

## Meeting Zorg

I put on my special space suit and slowly opened the rocket door. Outside, everything was purple – the ground, the sky, even the air seemed to shimmer with purple light!

"H-Hello?" I called out nervously.

Then I heard a sound: **"Bleep bloop whistle!"**

I turned around and gasped! Standing before me was the most unusual creature I had ever seen. It was shaped like a big purple blob, but it had three eyes (arranged in a triangle!), four little arms, and it was floating about one metre above the ground!

"Oh my!" I said. "Are you... are you an alien?"

"Bleep bloop!" it said again, and then, somehow, I understood! The alien was trying to say hello!

"Hi! I'm Zara from Earth. What's your name?" I asked.

"My name is Zorg!" the alien replied, using telepathy to speak directly into my mind. "Welcome to Planet Zorg! I've never met anyone from another planet before!"

## Becoming Friends

Zorg wasn't scary at all – he was actually really friendly and funny! He showed me around his planet, and I couldn't believe how amazing it was:

- **Zorg's Home** – a crystal cave that glowed from the inside, filled with comfortable floating beds made of clouds
- **The Floating Gardens** – where food grew in mid-air, including fruits that tasted like pizza, chocolate, and birthday cake!
- **The Rainbow Rivers** – rivers that flowed upwards instead of downwards, creating beautiful waterfalls that went UP!
- **The Singing Mountains** – mountains that played music when the wind blew through them

"This is incredible!" I said. "But I have a problem, Zorg. My spaceship needs special purple crystals to fix the engine, and I don't know where to find them."

Zorg's three eyes brightened. "Oh! I know exactly where they are! But the crystals are guarded by the grumpy Space Cyclops who lives in the Dark Crater. We'll have to be very clever!"

## The Quest for Crystals

Together, Zorg and I set out on an adventure across Planet Zorg. Along the way, we:

- **Rode flying creatures** that looked like jellyfish but flew through the air
- **Solved purple puzzles** to pass through magical gates
- **Made friends with robot sentinels** by singing them a funny song
- **Danced our way past sleeping dragons** to sneak through a cave
- **Found the Space Cyclops** who turned out to just be lonely, not grumpy at all!

When we talked to the Space Cyclops, we discovered he was sad because he had no friends. Instead of fighting him, Zorg and I invited him to be our friend! The Cyclops was so happy that he gave us the purple crystals freely and even helped us carry them back to my rocket.

## Going Home (But Never Forgetting)

With the crystals fixed, my rocket engine hummed happily again. But I felt sad saying goodbye to my new friend Zorg.

"Will I ever see you again?" I asked.

"Of course!" Zorg said, his eyes glowing warmly. "Friends are always connected, no matter how far apart they are in the universe. Plus, I can contact you through my telepathy across space! And who knows? Maybe you'll come visit me again!"

I hugged Zorg (which was a bit squishy but very nice), and the Space Cyclops gave me a great big hug too.

As my rocket blasted off from Planet Zorg, I watched the purple planet shrink in my window. But I knew I would never forget my adventure, and I would always have a friend waiting for me among the stars.

**THE END** 🌟
