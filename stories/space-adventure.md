## Mission Log — Day 1

My name is **Zara** and I am the youngest astronaut in the history of the Space Academy. Today was supposed to be a routine training mission around the outer planets.

It did not stay routine for very long.

## The Crash

Everything was going perfectly. My rocket — the *Silver Star* — hummed smoothly through the blackness of space. Stars twinkled all around like a billion tiny diamonds.

Then the alarm went off.

**BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!**

"Engine malfunction detected," said my ship's computer in a calm voice. "Emergency landing in three… two… one…"

The next thing I knew, I was looking up at a **purple sky** from the cracked window of my crashed rocket. Purple sky. Purple ground. Even the air seemed to shimmer with violet light.

*Where on Earth — well, where in the universe — was I?*

## First Contact

I put on my space suit, took three deep breaths, and stepped outside.

The planet was stunning. Strange crystal formations grew from the ground like trees. Bioluminescent plants glowed softly in rainbow colours. Three tiny moons hung low in the purple sky.

Then I heard a sound: **"Bleep bloop whistle chirp!"**

I spun around. Standing behind me was the most extraordinary creature I had ever seen.

It was about my height, shaped like a friendly blob, with three triangular eyes, four small arms, and was floating about a metre off the ground. Its skin shifted between blue, green and gold as it moved.

It looked just as surprised to see me as I was to see it.

"Hello," I said carefully. "My name is Zara. I come in peace."

A long pause. Then, impossibly, I heard a voice inside my head — gentle and warm and full of curiosity.

*"Hello, Zara. I am Zorg. This is Planet Zorg. I named it after myself. Is that strange?"*

"A little," I admitted. Then we both laughed.

## Exploring Planet Zorg

Zorg offered to show me around while we figured out how to fix my rocket. The planet was even more incredible up close:

🏔️ **The Crystal Mountains** — mountains made entirely of purple amethyst that rang like bells when the wind blew

🌺 **The Floating Gardens** — islands of flowers that drifted slowly through the air at head height

🎵 **The Echo Caves** — caves that recorded sounds and played them back as beautiful music

🌊 **The Rainbow Rivers** — rivers that flowed upward, creating reverse waterfalls that shot into the sky

## The Problem

My rocket needed **Zorinium crystals** — a rare mineral found only in the Glowing Caves — to repair the engine. But the caves were guarded by the Space Cyclops, a giant one-eyed creature who scared everyone away.

"He's actually not mean," Zorg told me quietly. "He's just… very large. And very misunderstood."

"Then let's go talk to him," I said.

## The Space Cyclops

The Space Cyclops was enormous — three times as tall as my rocket — with one giant purple eye and a voice like distant thunder. When he saw us approaching, he bellowed and stamped his enormous feet.

But I walked forward calmly and waved. "Hello! My name is Zara. I'm not scared of you. Are you okay? You look like you might be lonely."

The Cyclops stopped. His eye blinked. Once. Twice.

*"...How did you know?"* he rumbled softly.

"Because you're here all alone," I said, "and the louder someone shouts, the more they usually need a friend."

The Space Cyclops sat down heavily on the ground. He gave us all the Zorinium crystals we needed. In return, we spent the afternoon playing games with him and telling him stories about Earth and all the other wonderful places in the universe.

## Goodbye, Zorg

With the crystals in place, the *Silver Star* hummed back to life, engines glowing bright blue. My heart felt heavy.

"Will I ever see you again?" I asked Zorg.

*"Distance means nothing when hearts are connected,"* Zorg said, placing two of his small hands on my helmet. *"And I can send thoughts across the stars. You will always hear me."*

As my rocket climbed away from Planet Zorg, I looked down at the purple world growing smaller below me. Zorg and the Space Cyclops stood together, waving.

I waved back until they were just specks of light, and then not even that.

But I could still feel them there, warm and close, somewhere among the stars.

**THE END** 🌟
