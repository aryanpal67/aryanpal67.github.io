## The Gift Nobody Asked For

**Rohan** discovered he could turn invisible on a Tuesday.

It happened in the middle of a maths test, when Mrs. Patterson called on him to answer a question and he didn't know the answer. His cheeks went bright red, his ears burned — and then he simply wasn't there anymore.

Twenty-two classmates stared at an empty chair.

## Learning the Rules

By the end of the week, Rohan had figured out the rules:

1. He turned invisible when he felt **embarrassed** or **scared**
2. He stayed invisible until he felt **calm**
3. He could still be heard if he spoke — but only if he spoke loudly enough
4. Animals could always see him. Dogs were particularly unimpressed.

At first, it seemed wonderful. No more embarrassment. No more awkward moments. He could slip away from anything uncomfortable.

He ate his lunch invisible for three days straight.

## The Problem with Disappearing

But things started to go wrong.

His best friend Neel stopped saving him a seat at lunch because he never knew if Rohan would show up. He missed being picked for the school play because the drama teacher couldn't see him during auditions. He sat invisible in the back of the classroom and found, to his surprise, that not being seen felt exactly like not existing.

Worse — he started disappearing even when he *didn't* want to. A kind girl named Priya tried to share her lunch with him. His cheeks went warm with surprise and thankfulness — and *poof*.

"I know you're there," Priya said calmly to the air beside her. She held out half her sandwich. "You can come back whenever you're ready. No rush."

## The Choice

That afternoon, Rohan sat behind the school by himself, thinking hard.

He had spent so long hiding from awkward moments that he had missed out on all the warm ones too. Being invisible hadn't made him free. It had just made him… alone.

He took a deep breath.

He walked back to where Priya and Neel were sitting and sat down between them. His heart hammered. His cheeks glowed red. He waited for the familiar vanishing feeling—

—but this time, he didn't let it take him.

"I'm sorry I kept disappearing," he said in a small voice.

Neel grinned and shoved a crisp into his mouth. "I knew it was you eating my crisps that whole time."

Priya laughed so hard that juice came out of her nose, which made all three of them laugh even harder.

Rohan stayed visible for the rest of the day. It was the best day he could remember.

## Epilogue

Rohan never entirely lost the ability to turn invisible. But he used it less and less, because he discovered something better than disappearing.

Being seen — even when it was scary — meant being real.

And being real was the best thing in the world.
