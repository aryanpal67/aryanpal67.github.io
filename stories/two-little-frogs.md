Once upon a time, there were two little frogs named **Zara** and **Lilly**. They lived near a beautiful green pond surrounded by tall grass and colourful mushrooms. Zara was the elder frog, and Lilly was the younger one.

The two frogs always fought with each other. They never agreed on anything — not what to eat, not where to swim, not even which lily pad was the best one to sit on!

## 🍄 The Mushroom Field

One sunny morning, Lilly went to the mushroom field to collect mushrooms for dinner. Zara stayed near the pond for a quiet walk.

The mushroom field was beautiful — big round mushrooms of red and orange and yellow, with little spots and stripes. Lilly hopped happily from mushroom to mushroom, choosing the best ones.

Then suddenly — **a big snake appeared!**

It slithered out from behind the tall grass, its long body curling and its forked tongue flickering. The snake stared right at Lilly with its cold, yellow eyes.

Lilly's heart jumped. She quickly hid under the largest mushroom she could find, pressing herself flat against the ground and holding her breath. She stayed very, very still.

After what felt like forever, the snake slowly slithered away into the bushes. Lilly's legs were shaking as she hopped all the way home — without the mushrooms.

## 🏠 Not Believed

Zara was waiting at the pond. She frowned when she saw Lilly come back empty-handed.

**"Why are you so late, Lilly?"** Zara asked.

**"A big snake chased me in the mushroom field!"** Lilly said breathlessly. "It was enormous — I had to hide under a mushroom until it went away!"

But Zara crossed her front legs and smiled in a teasing way.

**"Don't fool me, Lilly,"** she said. **"You just forgot to bring the mushrooms for dinner!"**

Lilly felt a wave of sadness wash over her. She had been so scared, and now her own sister didn't believe her. She stayed quiet and went to bed that night with an empty tummy and a heavy heart.

## 🐍 The Snake Returns

The very next morning, Zara went for her usual walk near the swamp. The air was cool and misty, and the frogs sat on lily pads nearby.

Suddenly — **the same big snake came out from the bushes!**

It was even closer this time. Zara could see the scales gleaming in the sunlight. Her eyes went wide.

She ran as fast as her little legs could carry her — faster than she had ever run before. She dived behind a thick clump of leaves and crouched down, completely still.

The snake looked left. It looked right. It seemed confused. Then, slowly, it turned and disappeared back into the swamp.

Zara's heart was pounding like a drum. She stayed behind the leaves for a long, long time before she was sure it was really gone.

## 💚 Sorry and Forgiven

Zara hurried back home as fast as she could. Lilly was sitting quietly by the pond.

Zara took a deep breath.

**"I am sorry, Lilly,"** she said softly. **"I did not believe you yesterday. You were telling the truth, and I laughed at you. Please forgive me."**

Lilly looked at her sister's face — it was full of real sadness and genuine regret.

Then Lilly smiled her kindest smile.

**"It's okay, Zara,"** she said warmly. **"Sometimes mistakes happen. But from now on, we should trust each other and be good friends — always."**

Zara hopped over and hugged Lilly tight.

From that day on, Zara and Lilly stopped fighting. They collected mushrooms together, they swam together, and whenever one of them was scared, the other was always there.

They became the very best of friends — forever.
