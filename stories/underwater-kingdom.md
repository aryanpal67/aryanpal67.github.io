## Overboard!

It happened in an instant.

**Mia** was leaning too far over the edge of her grandfather's little fishing boat, trying to spot a dolphin she had seen in the distance, when — *whoops* — she tumbled overboard with a tremendous splash.

She plunged down, down, down through the blue-green water, bubbles swirling all around her.

Then something magical happened. Instead of running out of breath, she found she could breathe perfectly. A shimmer of light surrounded her, and she realised with a gasp that a tiny glowing fish was swimming in circles around her.

"Don't be scared," said the fish — in a perfectly ordinary voice, as if talking fish were the most normal thing in the world. "My name is Pip. The queen has been expecting you."

## The City of Coralia

At the bottom of the sea, Mia discovered a city unlike anything she had ever imagined.

**Coralia** was a magnificent underwater kingdom built from coral in every colour — rose pink, deep purple, vibrant orange, electric blue. Giant clamshells served as houses. Sea anemones swayed like colourful trees. Bioluminescent jellyfish drifted overhead like lanterns, lighting the streets in soft rainbow glow.

The citizens were extraordinary:

🐬 **Dolphins** who served as the royal messengers, leaping playfully between buildings

🐙 **Octopuses** who worked as architects, using all eight arms to build and decorate with incredible speed

🦀 **Crabs** who were the bakers, scuttling around enormous underwater kitchens

🦈 **Small, gentle sharks** who acted as the town guard, wearing tiny golden badges

## Queen Marina

At the centre of Coralia stood the Crystal Palace, built from a million fragments of sea glass ground smooth by centuries of waves. On her throne of pearl and coral sat **Queen Marina** — half human, half sea creature, with hair like dark seaweed and eyes like deep water.

"At last," the queen said warmly. "We have been waiting a long time for someone from the surface to come."

"Why me?" Mia asked.

"Because you are kind to the sea," the queen said. "You never litter. You always put rubbish in the bin. Small things — but the sea notices everything."

## The Pollution Problem

Queen Marina showed Mia the edge of the kingdom, where dark shadows gathered. Floating above the seabed was an enormous cloud of rubbish — plastic bags, bottles, cans — drifting down from the surface world.

"It is killing our coral," said the queen sadly. "The fish are getting sick. We cannot fix it from here. We need someone on the surface to tell people the truth."

Mia thought for a long moment. "I'll tell everyone," she said fiercely. "I promise."

## The Journey Home

Pip guided Mia back to the surface, swimming up through shafts of golden sunlight. Just before she broke the surface, Queen Marina's voice echoed gently through the water:

*"Remember your promise, Mia. The sea is listening."*

Mia burst through the surface gasping, and her grandfather's strong hands pulled her back into the boat. She was soaking wet, confused, and absolutely certain of one thing.

She had a promise to keep.

## Making a Change

Back at school, Mia gave a presentation about the ocean and pollution. She started a clean-up club. She wrote letters to her local council. She organised a beach clean with her whole class and collected seventeen bags of litter in a single morning.

Her town started recycling properly for the first time. The local beach won an award for being the cleanest in the region.

And sometimes, when Mia walked along the shore and the light caught the water just right, she would see a shimmer below the surface — and a tiny glowing fish would wink up at her.
