## The Old Shed

Every summer, Aryan visited his grandmother's house in the countryside. It was his favourite place in the world — it smelled of spices and old books, and there was always something interesting to discover.

This summer, he found the bicycle.

It was tucked right at the back of the garden shed, behind rusty garden tools and a broken lawnmower. It was old — really old — with a brass bell, worn leather seat, and handlebars engraved with tiny stars and moons.

Most extraordinarily, the front wheel was a clockface.

## Tick, Tick, WHOOOOSH

Aryan turned the hands of the clock to 66 million years ago — just to see what would happen — and pedalled as fast as he could.

The shed blurred. The garden blurred. Everything blurred into a tunnel of spinning golden light.

Then with a tremendous **BOOM**, everything stopped.

Aryan stepped off the bicycle and stared.

He was in a jungle the size of a continent. The trees were enormous — taller than buildings. The air was warm and thick and smelled like rain and earth. And walking through the undergrowth, not thirty metres away, was a **Triceratops** the size of a bus.

"Oh," said Aryan. "Oh WOW."

## Dinosaur Friends

The Triceratops turned to look at him. It snuffled, flicked its great horned head, and went back to eating leaves.

Over the next hour, Aryan saw:

🦕 **A Brachiosaurus** stretching its impossibly long neck to munch treetops

🦖 **A young T-Rex** (thankfully, a very small one) who sniffed him curiously and then bounded away

🐦 **Pterodactyls** wheeling overhead like enormous prehistoric kites

🥚 **A dinosaur nest** with three enormous eggs, being guarded by a watchful mother

Aryan sketched everything in his notebook, his pencil flying across the page.

## The Very Close Call

Then he heard a sound that made his blood run cold.

Not a small T-Rex this time.

A very, very big one.

Aryan ran for the bicycle faster than he had ever run in his life, leapt onto the seat, twisted the clock hands to **present day**, and pedalled furiously.

The jungle blurred. Time collapsed. He burst back into his grandmother's garden shed with a shower of golden sparks, gasping and laughing and absolutely buzzing with excitement.

## More Adventures

Over the rest of that summer, Aryan visited:

- 🏺 **Ancient Egypt**, where he helped an architect double-check his pyramid measurements
- 🏰 **Medieval England**, where he accidentally ended up in a jousting tournament
- 🌕 **The future Moon colony of 2150**, where children went to school in zero gravity
- 🎨 **Renaissance Florence**, where a very surprised Leonardo da Vinci thought Aryan's modern pencil was the most remarkable invention he had ever seen

Each time, Aryan came home with a notebook stuffed with drawings and a head full of stories.

## The Secret

At the end of summer, before he packed the bicycle carefully away for next year, Aryan asked his grandmother how long she had owned it.

His grandmother smiled mysteriously.

"It has been in the family for a very, very long time," she said. "Your great-great-great-grandmother brought it back from somewhere she never quite explained. I always found it too frightening to ride myself."

She looked at Aryan's bulging sketchbook.

"But I always hoped someone braver than me would find it."

**THE END** 🚲✨
